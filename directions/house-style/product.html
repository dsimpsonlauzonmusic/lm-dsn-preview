Provenance

Why this one made the wall

We picked this one off the Custom Shop wall ourselves, at the Fender Summer Dealer Event in Corona. A room full of builds, and this was the one that kept getting handed around. Super Heavy Relic ash in Faded Aged Desert Sand, checked and worn the way a real '51 goes after seventy years of bar gigs, with the kind of honest arm wear and edge burn the Custom Shop only gets right when they take their time.

The neck is the argument for blackguards: quarter-sawn roasted maple carved to the Nocaster "U", a genuinely big handful that settles into the palm instead of fighting it. A 9.5" radius and 6150 medium-jumbos keep it modern where it counts, so it bends clean and plays in tune up the board. The handwound Nocaster pickups are the point of the whole build: raw and pushed in the bridge, round and woody in the neck, the sound everyone chasing an original blackguard is actually chasing.

One build, one serial: R131233, photographed exactly as it sits. Before it ships, it crosses our tech's bench for a final setup to your spec, and it leaves in its Custom Shop hardshell with the certificate and full case candy. If you have been circling a Nocaster, this is the one to call about.
